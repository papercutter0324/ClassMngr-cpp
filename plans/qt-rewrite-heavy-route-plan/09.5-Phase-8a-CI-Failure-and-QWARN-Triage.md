## Acceptance

Phase 8a is complete only when:

- Every host has a clean, stable source fingerprint at the start and end of the baseline run, with source provenance that excludes generated/dependency installation state or records a verified source snapshot.
- C53 capture is produced after a successful build even when unrelated CTest tests fail; a failed build still blocks capture. The capture step reports its own result.
- Required Schedule, Roster, Speaking, and C53 evidence passes on macOS, Windows x64, and Linux.
- Every direct Phase 8 failure below is fixed and verified or dispositioned with retained evidence; every failure owned by a later phase has an explicit handoff. Target-only observations remain unresolved until case-level evidence is obtained.
- Every QWARN family and occurrence set has a tested or explicitly owned disposition. Preserve assertions, failure visibility, and warning output; do not suppress warnings or weaken checks.

This acceptance does not mean the full CTest suite passes. Record focused results as focused and keep unrelated failures visible.
